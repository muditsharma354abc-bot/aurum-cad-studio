Process

  1. 1. Brief

    Share references, metal, ring size and stone sizes.

  2. 2. CAD

    Precise modelling in Matrix 9 / Rhino.

  3. 3. QC

    Checks on dimensions, wall thickness, stones and closed mesh.

  4. 4. Renders & STL

    Photoreal renders for approval, then STL files.

  5. 5. Delivery

    Final STL, 3DM and renders, with revisions as agreed.

FAQ

What do you need to start?Reference images or sketch, metal type, ring size (if a ring), and stone shapes/sizes.
Which file formats do I get?STL for casting/printing, 3DM (Rhino/Matrix) for editing, and rendered images.
Can you modify an existing file?Yes — resizing, stone changes and design edits.
How do I pay?Through Fiverr, Freelancer or Upwork for buyer protection.
